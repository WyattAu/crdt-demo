crdt-demo — everyone types, everyone converges

Open this page in two or more tabs and type in all of them at once. Every keystroke becomes an RGA operation; every replica converges on the same text — no locks, no merge conflicts, no last-write-wins. Name yourself by appending ?token=Alice to the URL.

Powered by crdts-kit (RGA text CRDT) and ws-kit (WebSocket broadcast rooms) on axum — one Rust binary, zero framework in the browser. Keyboard undo/redo and IME are out of scope for the demo.
